## 2. Can machine learning beat Elo?

Chronological split after a 90-day burn-in; both teams need 5 earlier games.

| Split | Period | Games | Blue win % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Train | 2 Apr 2020 to 11 Aug 2024 | 47,070 | 52.7% |
| Validation | 11 Aug 2024 to 23 Sep 2025 | 10,086 | 53.4% |
| Test | 23 Sep 2025 to 14 Nov 2026 | 10,087 | 53.5% |

Elo now includes **league offsets** (version 2 of the benchmark, section 5). Chosen on
validation: K = 40, season shrinkage 0.5, k_league = 16; logistic C = 0.1.

| Test period | Brier | Log loss | ROC-AUC | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flat Elo (first version) | 0.2230 | 0.6373 | 0.688 | 63.6% |
| Elo with league offsets | 0.2176 | 0.6249 | 0.705 | 64.7% |
| Calibrated Elo (with offsets) | 0.2170 | 0.6232 | 0.705 | 64.8% |
| Logistic regression (with offsets) | 0.2164 | 0.6215 | 0.707 | 64.9% |

Paired cluster bootstrap over 4,584 series (2,000 resamples), Brier difference:

| Comparison | Difference | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| Elo with league offsets vs flat Elo | -0.00543 | -0.00675 to -0.00406 |
| Same, only the 3,095 games between teams from different leagues | -0.01539 | -0.01952 to -0.01117 |
| Logistic vs Elo (with offsets) | -0.00118 | -0.00188 to -0.00047 |
| Calibrated Elo vs Elo (with offsets) | -0.00056 | -0.00110 to +0.00003 (includes zero) |
| Logistic vs calibrated Elo | -0.00062 | -0.00104 to -0.00023 |

Reading:
- Fixing how leagues relate to each other improves the benchmark by 2.4% of its Brier
  score, about four times what the machine-learning features add on top of it (0.54%).
  Modelling the structure of the competition matters more than adding predictors.
- The logistic regression still beats Elo, and the interval excludes zero, but the gain is
  small and accuracy barely moves (64.7% to 64.9%).
- With league offsets, the Elo coefficient rises (0.547 to 0.641 per SD) and recent form
  falls (0.134 to 0.083): part of what "recent form" captured before was mis-scaled
  cross-league ratings.
- Blue-side effect: with equal Elo the calibrated model gives blue 53.2%.

First-version results (flat Elo) are kept in the table for comparison: logistic vs flat Elo
was -0.00147 (CI -0.00232 to -0.00061).

## 4. Problems found on real data and what was changed

1. **Database load failed at full size** (query with more than 65,535 parameters). Fixed by
   joining on the game id instead of listing all match ids. The failed run was rolled back
   and logged.
2. **Grid edges.** In the first training run the selected K (40), shrinkage (0.5) and C (0.01)
   were all at the edge of their grids. The grids were widened (choice based on validation
   only); the same values were selected again, now inside the grid, and test results did not
   change.
3. **Bootstrap.** Changed from resampling single games to resampling series (games between
   the same two teams in the same tournament on the same UTC day). Intervals widened slightly;
   conclusions unchanged.
4. **Misleading trend.** The first balance report fitted a trend that included the "2027"
   season (6 splits, just started) and showed -0.051 per year. Trends now use seasons with at
   least 20 splits: -0.003.
5. **Cross-league Elo.** The first overall ranking put LFL and academy teams above Gen.G,
   because small leagues rarely play other leagues and their Elo sits on a separate scale.
   Fixed in two steps: (a) the default ranking covers only leagues with 50+ games at tier-1
   international events in the last three years (CBLOL, LCK, LCP, LCS, LEC, LPL, PCS, VCS);
   (b) Elo now has league offsets (section 5). The top 10 is now Bilibili Gaming, Gen.G, T1,
   Hanwha Life, DN SOOPers, Dplus Kia, Invictus Gaming, BNK FEARX, Top Esports, Ninjas in Pyjamas.
6. Derived champions spot-checked for LEC and LCK 2020-2024 against known results; they match.
   Newer formats (LCK "Cup", "Rounds 1-2") do not map cleanly to titles.

## 5. Elo with league offsets

Each team's rating = league level + team component. Games between leagues move the two
league levels by k_league x (result - expectation); new teams start at their league's level;
a team's home league is the one it has played most domestic games in so far. k_league = 0 is
exactly flat Elo (tested).

Validation log loss by k_league (K = 40, shrinkage 0.5): 0 → 0.6415, 2 → 0.6375,
4 → 0.6356, 8 → 0.6337, 16 → 0.6329 (selected), 32 → 0.6343.

League levels (offset from 1500) for leagues linked through international events:
LCK +471, LPL +395, LEC +213, LCS +188, LCP +84, CBLOL +69, VCS and PCS slightly below 0.

Limitation: leagues that meet the major leagues rarely (European regional leagues, LCK
Challengers) get levels that are identified mainly against their neighbours. The LFL, for
example, gets +432 because it dominates European Masters, not because it is comparable to the
LPL. The app shows these as "weakly linked" and keeps them out of the default ranking.
